# Product LifeCycle

A web app that helps small product teams track a product from initial concept through launch, maintenance, and retirement.

- Organize products into lifecycle stages with clear owners, milestones, and transition criteria.
- Centralize customer feedback, requirements, and launch checklists on each product’s timeline.
- Track adoption, revenue, and support costs to inform investment or retirement decisions.
- Notify stakeholders of upcoming deadlines, stalled milestones, and stage changes.
- Generate a lifecycle report summarizing key decisions, performance trends, and lessons learned.
